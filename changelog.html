RELEASE HISTORY

What's new?

Track Lens is still a beta. Updates focus on useful DJ analysis and accurate recording matching.

v0.8.2 · 24 October 2026

  • Harmonic matches and other-key related tracks display in separate, clearly labelled sections.
  • Advanced Spotify Queue diagnostics no longer appear on YouTube Music.
  • Scan New Tracks finds fresh recommendations only; Retry Failed Lookups requests missing or failed analyses again.
  • Dismiss incorrect catalogue review suggestions for the current seed track and restore them later. Dismissals are saved locally and included in exports.

v0.8.1 · 24 October 2026

  • Added dark/light themes, saved per browser profile.
  • Larger desktop typography and a wider panel.
  • Desktop draggable header and keyboard movement with saved positions.
  • Separate desktop and mobile panel positioning, with reset settings.

v0.8.0 · 23 October 2026

  • Beta distribution/privacy preparations.
  • Local export and import of saved analyses, preferences and manual metrics.

v0.7.1 · 22 October 2026

  • Clearer reasons for uncertain catalogue matches.
  • Mobile-friendly recording review controls and staged review list.

Known limitations

  • Some remixes or lesser-known recordings need manual confirmation or entered metrics.
  • Song Finder and ReccoBeats catalogue coverage and scores vary.
  • Unpacked installations require manual updates.
  • Site-player layout updates may affect track detection or recommendations.
