THE USEFUL BITS

What it does

Mostly, it helps you find a good next tune without losing your place in the music.

↗

Find tracks that mix

Checks Spotify Radio or Queue, or YouTube Music Up Next and Related, for compatible Camelot keys. Songs in other keys are listed separately.

♬

The numbers, right there

See BPM, Camelot key, energy and danceability next to the player, instead of opening another tab.

◈

Move it out of the way

Drag the panel around on desktop, use the smaller strip on mobile, or switch between light and dark mode.

◎

Keep an eye on energy

See energy out of 100 while listening and comparing possible next tracks. Scores can vary between data sources.

≋

Pick the right version

If the catalogue isn't sure about an Original, Extended or Remix version, you can check and choose the recording.

▣

Remember what you've checked

Track Lens keeps analysed tracks and corrections in your browser, with backup and restore in Settings.

DATA SOURCES

Where the track info comes from

Track Lens doesn't analyse the music audio itself. It looks up recording data from these services, so some tracks or remixes won't have results.

Song Finder ↗

The main source for BPM, key, energy and danceability when the recording has analysis available.

ReccoBeats ↗

A fallback for missing analysis when Track Lens can identify the correct recording. Its scores may differ from Song Finder's.

Track Lens is independent of both providers. The links above go to their official API documentation. See what data is shared.

GETTING STARTED

Install it in Chrome or Vivaldi

It isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so you'll load it yourself. Updates are manual for now too.

  1. Download and extract the ZIPKeep the extracted track-lens folder in a permanent location. Avoid moving or renaming it after installation.
  2. Open your browser's extension managerChrome: chrome://extensions · Vivaldi: vivaldi://extensions. Switch on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpackedSelect the extracted track-lens folder (the one containing manifest.json). Open Spotify Web Player or YouTube Music and play a song.
Full installation and update guide ↗

Good to know

  • It works on the Spotify and YouTube Music websites in your browser, not inside their native apps.
  • Some recordings need a manual match, and some won't have analysis available yet.
  • Compatible keys are a starting point. Listen to the phrasing and feel before mixing.
  • It doesn't record audio, need your streaming passwords, or show adverts.
Still a beta: Expect the occasional missing track or manual update. Only install extensions from sources you trust.
LATEST CHANGES

What changed in v0.8.2?

Harmonic matches stay separate

Tracks in compatible Camelot keys have their own list. Related tracks in other keys are clearly labelled, not passed off as harmonic matches.

Less fiddling around

Scan newly loaded songs, retry failed lookups and dismiss incorrect recording suggestions. You can also drag the panel and switch themes.

Manually installed betas need manual updates: replace the files in the same installation folder, then reload the extension. Read the update guide.
