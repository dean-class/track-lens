Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026 · Track Lens browser extension v0.8.2

The short version

Track Lens reads visible public song metadata on supported music-player websites, sends recording identifiers to music-analysis services when needed, and stores your analysed tracks and preferences locally. It does not capture music audio or require your streaming-service passwords.

What Track Lens reads

On open.spotify.com and music.youtube.com, Track Lens may read the current song's title, artist credit and public recording ID, plus titles and artists in visible Radio, Queue, Related and Up Next sections when you request harmonic suggestions. It operates on these music sites, not across unrelated pages.

What is shared with third parties

To provide acoustic analysis, public song titles, artist names, ISRCs and publicly visible Spotify recording IDs may be sent over HTTPS to Song Finder and, as a fallback, ReccoBeats. On-demand recommendations cause selected visible candidate titles and artist names to be looked up. These services may process IP addresses and request metadata under their own policies. MusicBrainz opens only when you choose its link.

What stays on your device

Track Lens stores cached analysis, manually verified recording selections, manually entered metrics and source references, seed-specific dismissed review choices, panel theme and panel positions in browser extension storage. You can export or import a JSON library backup in Help & Settings. Backups remain on your device unless you share them.

Data not collected

The extension does not record playback audio, read your Spotify or Google login passwords, access browsing history on unrelated websites, or operate a developer-managed analytics or advertising service. The GitHub Pages download site itself contains no custom analytics or tracking scripts in this release. GitHub may process routine hosting/access logs under its own policies.

Retention and deletion

Cached analyses can expire for lookup purposes while stored entries remain until replaced or deleted. Manual metrics, dismissal choices and preferences stay until you clear them. Use Help & Settings → Clear cache to remove saved track matches/analyses/manual metrics while keeping display preferences. Remove the extension to remove its local extension data; remove any exported backup files separately.

Publisher and contact

Track Lens is an independently developed beta. For support or privacy enquiries, use the Track Lens issue tracker. GitHub Issues are public: do not submit private listening-library backups, passwords, account information or personal data. If an enquiry requires private information, please do not post it in a public issue. No developer-hosted personal-data database is operated for this release.

Changes to this policy

The policy may change when features or third-party services change. Material changes will be published alongside release notes. This page and extension should be reviewed again before a wider launch.
